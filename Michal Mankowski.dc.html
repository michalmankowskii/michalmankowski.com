I develop computational methods to improve organ allocation, use large-scale clinical data to understand how care is delivered, and study how AI can reshape patient care and communication.

Research
01

Optimizing access to organ transplant

Allocation policy as an engineering problem: matching, equity, and utility across the national kidney system.

02

Learning from electronic medical records

Real-world evidence at national scale, used to answer questions trials were never built to answer.

03

How AI will shape healthcare delivery and patient experience

What changes for clinicians, for systems, and for the person waiting on the other side of the decision.

04

Understanding humanity, and teaching medicine, through art

Art as a way into the parts of medicine that data cannot reach, and as a teaching instrument.
